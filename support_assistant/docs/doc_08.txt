doc_08 — Customer Support Hours: "Zepto customer support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week through the in-app Help & Support section. Customers can raise support requests at any time for issues including missing items, damaged products, incorrect items, payment problems, delivery delays, and account-related concerns. For urgent issues related to an active order, customers should contact support from the active order screen so that the support team can access the relevant order details."
