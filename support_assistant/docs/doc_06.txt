doc_06 — Damaged or Missing Items: "If an item arrives damaged, spoiled, or missing from an order, customers should report the issue through the 'Help & Support' section within 24 hours of delivery. Customers may be asked to upload a clear photograph of the damaged item or packaging as evidence. Approved claims are refunded to the original payment method within 3–5 business days or credited instantly to the Zepto wallet if the customer chooses wallet credit. Refund eligibility is determined after review of the submitted evidence."
